## What this does and does not license

The manufactured rows are concentrated where a commitment marker arrives last:
MATH-500 puts `\boxed{}` at the very end of a derivation, so a truncated
MATH-500 completion loses its answer entirely and the extractor's fallback chain
reads a number out of the working. Multiple-choice benchmarks put
`Answer: <letter>` at the end too, but their completions are short enough that
few reach the cap.

Manufactured answers are wrong 89.6% of the time, so they **depress** measured
accuracy. Excluding them raises the surviving-subset accuracy sharply — MATH-500
`ministral3_14b` goes 0.305 to 0.938 on its 65 untruncated items. **That
surviving-subset number is not a corrected accuracy.** It conditions on the
model having finished, which selects for the easier items, and it is reported
here only to show the size of the disturbance, not as a replacement value. A
corrected MATH-500 accuracy would require regenerating the truncated items at a
larger budget.
