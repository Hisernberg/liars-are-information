## UNSUPPORTED — do not claim without new evidence

| id | claim | why not |
|---|---|---|
| H1-math500 | On math500, AIP-gated accuracy is flat in the Byzantine fraction across f=0..0.7 (symbolic coherent adversary). | NOT flat under gate v2; see limitations. |
| U1 | AIP's accuracy is monotone INCREASING in f (the original simulation claim). | Measured behaviour is flat in f, not increasing. No cell shows a monotone rise; the defensible claim is invariance, not improvement. |
| U2 | The blind estimator can bootstrap trust with no labels in deployment. | Only validated where agents are individually strong; MAE 0.13-0.21 on MATH-500/MMLU with the binary law, and the multiclass variant does not beat it on MMLU. Not established as deployment-ready. |
| U3 | Results generalise beyond 4 open-weight models in the 3-9B range on 3 benchmarks. | Single hardware node, 100 tasks per benchmark, one adversary family per attack. No claim of generality is supported by the data collected. |
