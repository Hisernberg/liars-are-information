\begin{abstract}
Byzantine fault tolerance for multi-agent systems is built on discarding, and the
resulting 50\% ceiling is a property of discarding rather than of the problem.
Correlated error is known to cap
evaluation panels; we confirm that ceiling in an adversarial swarm and extend it
to the replicated-model case a deployment actually builds
($\numWithinModelPhi$ within-model against $\numCrossModelPhi$ cross-family),
with a calibrated law rather than an observation. Over all
$\numCompSubsetsThree$ three-model subsets of our roster no composition beats
its own strongest single member by a detectable margin, and on MATH-500 every
subset is worse by at least $\numCompBestGainMathfive$: an LLM swarm's ceiling on
honest error is \emph{correlation, not competence}. The only structure in the
broadcast set that honest agents do not already share is therefore
\emph{adversarial} coherence: a disciplined liar is a swarm's one informative
contributor, and can be inverted rather than filtered.

The obstacle is that honest models also agree on wrong answers
($\numHonestQMmlu$ on four-way multiple choice against chance
$\numMmluChanceQ$), so we gate inversion on a per-answer-space calibrated
threshold estimated blind via a multiclass attenuation law exact at $C=2$. Across
$\numAttackClasses$ attack classes and $\numBenchmarks$ benchmarks with
prompt-injected adversaries, gated inversion is worth $\numInversionGainFseven$
at $f=0.7$ and costs $\numInversionCostFzero$ --- below the floor --- at $f=0$.

We then defeat our own mechanism. An adversary given the published calibration
constant coordinates just beneath it, driving accuracy to $\numEvasionAccMedqa$
at the exact tie; its optimum coherence, $\numEvasionQMedqa$, lies \emph{below}
the chance value $\numEvasionChance$ at which honest agents collide by accident.
No rule measurable with respect to per-question coherence separates the two. One
finding outlives the mechanism: the logprob confidence surface discriminates
correctness better than the self-report ($\numAucLogprob$ against
$\numAucSelfReport$) and cannot be authored by the sender, whose falsified
self-report drops below chance to $\numAucSelfReportFalsified$. Ten claims this
study set out to confirm were refuted by its own data, and are reported at the
same length as the confirmations.
\end{abstract}
