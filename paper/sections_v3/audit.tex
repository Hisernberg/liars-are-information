\section{Audit of the prior artifact}\label{sec:audit}

A journal-grade result on cached model outputs is only as sound as the cache and the code that reads it. Before building on the prior releases~\citep{das2026aip,nabidnur2026bucket} we audited them. Every item below was verified by running code or diffing files.

\begin{description}[style=unboxed,leftmargin=0pt]
\item[Truncation (X3).] In the original cache, 944 of 13{,}500 answers were \emph{manufactured}: a 640/2048-token budget truncated completions, and the extractor then read a number out of the unfinished working, at a 100\% parse rate. The upstream repair regenerated 2{,}313 rows. Our loader reproduces the repaired single-model table exactly. All our results use the repaired cache. MATH-500 conclusions in the earlier derivative release, which predates the repair, are superseded. For example, three strong models gained 26--50 points on MATH-500.
\item[Incomplete recompute.] After the repair, four result files that the manuscript reads (\texttt{n\_scaling}, \texttt{minimax\_regret}, \texttt{accuracy\_landscape}, \texttt{gate\_aware\_mitigations}) are byte-identical to their pre-repair versions. All 54{,}432 frozen-roster rows of the aggregation sweep are value-identical, because the sweep resumed and skipped existing cells. The headline macros of the upstream manuscript are therefore pre-repair. A fresh recompute of the gate-aware anchor gives 0.087/0.889 where the paper prints 0.082/0.878. We re-derive every number from scratch.
\item[Code defects.] With $p_{\text{obs}}<1$ the upstream \aip\ fit paired answers from \emph{different} questions. It seeded the randomised threshold with Python's salted \texttt{hash()}, so runs were not reproducible across processes. It kept stale state across refits, and it counted missing answers as disagreements. The derivative release fixed all four. We build on the fixed code, and all our seeds are SHA-256-derived.
\item[Nondeterminism.] Regenerated Llama-3.2-3B rows flip their extracted answer in 52 of 300 re-runs (17.3\%) at identical settings. We report this and treat the regenerated rows as fresh samples.
\item[Pipeline extension (MX).] Same-model ``replicas'' were byte-identical because one seed was shared across the batch, so the negative controls passed trivially. The extension stopped before any liar ran. Our live experiment uses distinct models and explicit roles instead.
\end{description}
None of these defects changes the qualitative picture of \aip\ (inversion helps against a coherent bloc; the gate-aware attacker defeats it). All of them change numbers. The repaired, re-derived numbers are the ones reported here.
