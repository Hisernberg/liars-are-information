\section{Development log: changes made after inspecting data}\label{app:devlog}

A change was admissible only if all three of the following held:
\begin{enumerate}
\item it fixes a mechanism that can be stated without reference to a benchmark number;
\item it keeps every previously fixed hyper-parameter;
\item the superseded variant stays in every table.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Initial design.} Anchored one-coin EM with clone tempering by raw agreement $\ge0.95$ (kept as \texttt{race\_rawclone}).
\item \textbf{Rejected: multi-start MAP.} We ran EM from both the receiver's answers and the plurality and kept the higher log-posterior (\texttt{race\_ms}). Trigger: a near-chance MATH-500 receiver captured by a rushing liar. It did not help, because with a near-chance anchor the two labellings are likelihood-equivalent. It was harmful when replicated channels inflate the likelihood of the adversarial labelling. The anchor therefore acts through initialisation and constraint, not through a likelihood comparison.
\item \textbf{Adopted: two-part clone rule.} Same-source links (raw agreement $\ge0.99$, applied before any fit) and dependent-error links (agreement $\ge0.95$ on tasks where a member errs). Trigger: GSM8K with uninformative/partial liars at $f=0.7$, where raw-agreement grouping merged distinct accurate models and collapsed honest evidence (\cref{prop:clones}).
\end{enumerate}
The method was then frozen, and every study was re-run from scratch. One extension was added after the freeze, as a separate method that leaves \race\ unchanged: \raceD\ (\cref{sec:budget}), which fits channels on disagreement questions only. It was specified before E9 was run and is reported as a negative result. A second post-freeze change is the binary rule of \cref{sec:binary} (v3.1), adopted after the live swarm (E8) exposed option-biased channels; every study was re-run with it, and v3.0 is reported alongside (\cref{app:binary}). The replayed LLM deceivers (E3) and the live swarm (E8) had not been inspected at freeze time. On E3 the adopted rule outperforms both superseded variants at every $f\ge0.5$.

\section{Reproducibility}\label{app:repro}
\begin{sloppypar}
All code, data, results, figures, videos and this manuscript are in the accompanying repository and Hugging Face release. One command, \texttt{experiments/\allowbreak reproduce\_all.sh}, regenerates everything on a 4-core CPU without a GPU in about an hour: E1--E7 and E9 from the cached answers, the E8 evaluation from the live-swarm cache, every figure, table and number, the videos, the repository README and this PDF. It finishes by re-checking every qualitative claim in the text against the results (\texttt{experiments/\allowbreak verify\_claims.py}). \texttt{experiments/\allowbreak live\_swarm.py} regenerates the live answers themselves. The numbers in this paper are macros written by \texttt{experiments/\allowbreak make\_numbers.py}. Each study directory holds a \texttt{run\_manifest.json} with the input and code hashes, wall time and peak memory. The repository's test suite covers \race's identification, invariance, clone-grouping, class-conditional and causality properties.
\end{sloppypar}
