The sleeper is the one attack in the zoo that defeats every static defender, including the known-channel oracle, because it violates stationarity. We therefore re-ran five attacker schedules under a strictly causal protocol on MMLU, MedQA, BoolQ and MATH-500 ($f\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}$, two seeds; 120 streams of 200 questions). At each step every honest receiver predicts from a state fitted only on earlier questions, then commits the observation. Methods refit every 8 questions after a 40-question warm-up. A test confirms that future broadcasts or labels cannot change a past prediction.

\paragraph{Sleepers.} The sleeper answers honestly for 100 questions and then lies coherently. We average over the last 60 questions, which start 40 questions after the switch, and over the three values of $f$. A \race\ fit frozen at warm-up scores \onSleeperFixed\%. Cumulative refitting recovers to \onSleeperCum\%. Forgetting ($\gamma=0.97$) recovers to \onSleeperDecay\% and a 64-question window to \onSleeperWin\%, both above the receiver alone (\onSleeperSelf\%) and far above a windowed \aip\ (\onSleeperAipWin\%) and majority vote (\onSleeperMaj\%). At $f=0.7$ the frozen fit and majority vote both score \onSleeperSevenFixed\%, while forgetting scores \onSleeperSevenDecay\% (receiver alone: \onSleeperSevenSelf\%). Once the sleeper starts lying, it is a coherent bloc like any other, and the forgetting receiver learns to read it backwards within about 40 questions (\cref{fig:online}).

\paragraph{Regime switches and the price of forgetting.} When the bloc switches from coherent to independent lies, or back, forgetting scores \onCohIndDecay\% and \onIndCohDecay\% after the switch (cumulative: \onCohIndCum\% and \onIndCohCum\%; windowed \aip: \onCohIndAipWin\% and \onIndCohAipWin\%). A bloc that alternates between coherent lies and silence every 40 questions leaves \race\ at \onToggleDecay\%, against \onToggleMaj\% for majority vote. On a stationary coherent schedule, forgetting costs nothing measurable (\onStatDecay\% vs.\ \onStatCum\% cumulative). Forgetting is therefore the default we recommend when stationarity cannot be assumed, and it is exposed as \texttt{TrustLayer(forgetting=0.97)}.
