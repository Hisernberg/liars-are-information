Is the receiver anchor necessary, or would any mature crowdsourcing estimator do? E11 adds four classical label-free estimators to both Dawid--Skene variants: iterative weighted majority voting (IWMV)~\citep{li2014iwmv}, MACE~\citep{hovy2013}, GLAD~\citep{whitehill2009} and, on binary questions, the message-passing estimator of Karger, Oh and Shah (KOS)~\citep{karger2014}. The protocol is exactly that of \race. Each honest agent runs every estimator on its own HISTORY window of peer answers, no estimator sees a label, and ties go to the agent's own answer. E11 covers \nWorldsCrowd\ worlds: six benchmarks; a coherent bloc at $f\in\{0.1,0.3,0.5,0.7\}$; gate-aware liars ($p=0.25$) and the four LLM prompts at $f\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}$; and three seeds. \Cref{fig:crowd} shows the result.

\paragraph{With a liar minority the classical estimators are as good as \race.} At $f=0.3$ the best of the four estimators defined on every benchmark (IWMV, MACE, GLAD, label-free Dawid--Skene) scores \crowdBestClassicalThree\%, and \race\ \crowdRaceThree\%. Over the \crowdClassicalCellsLow\ comparisons between \race\ and a classical estimator at $f\le0.3$, \race\ ties in \crowdClassicalTiesLow\ and wins \crowdClassicalWinsLow. This is the regime these estimators were designed for, and they work.

\paragraph{With a liar majority every one of them collapses.} At $f=0.7$ the best of those four scores \crowdBestClassicalSeven\%, full-confusion Dawid--Skene \crowdDsFullSeven\% on the four categorical benchmarks, and majority vote \crowdMajSeven\%. \race\ scores \crowdRaceSeven\%, the known-channel oracle \crowdOracleSeven\% and the receiver alone \crowdSelfSeven\%. On BoolQ, KOS scores \crowdBoolqKosSeven\% against \race's \crowdBoolqRaceSeven\%. The failure is the label-switching symmetry of \cref{prop:switching}, not a weakness of any single estimator. The likelihood of a latent-class model is invariant to relabelling the truth. Without an anchor, EM settles on the relabelling in which the \emph{majority} is reliable, so it decodes the liars' labelling faithfully and inverts every honest agent. The receiver anchor breaks exactly this symmetry. Anchoring Dawid--Skene and nothing else gives \race\ without clone tempering, which E1--E3 already evaluate.

\paragraph{Paired tests.} There are \crowdClassicalCells\ (benchmark, $f$, attack) comparisons between \race\ and a classical estimator. \race\ wins \crowdClassicalWins\ and loses \crowdClassicalLosses, with Holm correction over all \crowdCells\ comparisons of the study. At $f\ge0.5$ it wins \crowdClassicalWinsHigh\ of \crowdClassicalCellsHigh. The classical estimators are therefore not stronger baselines than those of E1--E3. Their failure at $f\ge 1/2$ is the reason an anchored estimator is needed.
